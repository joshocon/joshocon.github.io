About

A little about me.

I'm a junior mathematics undergraduate at the University of Kansas, a McNair Scholar, and a first-generation college student. My research sits at the intersection of algebraic combinatorics and representation theory.

I work with Professor Jeremy Martin on chromatic symmetric functions, and I'm heading to MSU's SURIEM REU this summer to study combinatorial approaches to quantum sl₃ knot invariants.

Outside of research, I run the KU Math Club and work as a calculus SI leader — because I believe mathematics should be accessible to everyone.

Research Interests

01

Chromatic Symmetric Functions

Investigating the e-positivity conjecture and star basis expansions, with computational exploration of caterpillar graphs.

02

Quantum Knot Invariants

Combinatorial approaches to quantum sl₃ invariants via MOY calculus and the Temperley–Lieb algebra.

03

Symmetric Function Theory

Integer partitions, Young tableaux, and the rich interplay between representation theory and combinatorics.
