    About

    I'm a rising senior mathematics undergraduate at KU, a McNair Scholar, and a first-generation college student. I'm driven by a love for mathematical structure, and turning hard problems into simple ones.

    Research

    In the summer of 2025, I worked with Prof. Reuven Hodges on Kohnert diagrams, and their associate posets and polynomials. In summer of 2026, I traveled to Michigan State University to participate in the Summer Research Opportunities Program (SROP) and the SURIEM REU, where myself and a team of undergraduate researchers attemped to find combinatorial formulas for fourth and fifth coefficients of the quantum sl3 knot invariant. Going into my senior year, I will work with Prof. Jeremy Martin on chromatic symmetric functions, studying e-positivity and the Stanley conjecture, for my honors thesis.
