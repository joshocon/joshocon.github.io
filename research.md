<h1>Research</h1>

<h2>Interests</h2>
<p>
  My research sits at the intersection of algebraic combinatorics and representation theory.
  I'm particularly drawn to symmetric functions, integer partitions, Young tableaux, and generating functions. My favorite part of combinatorics is seeing polynomials arise from combinatorial structure.
</p>

<h2>Kohnert Diagrams, Polyomials, and Posets</h2>
<p>
 I worked with Dr. Reuven Hodges on studying when the poset associated with a southeast Kohnert diagram is ranked or bounded. 
  We also studied when the associated Kohnert polynomial was monomial-multiplicity free. This experience ended
  with two sufficient conditions for boundedness and monomial multiplicity freeness. 
  The main product of this work was code that generated all of these objects, as well as automatically writing TiKz to render the posets in LaTex.
</p>

<h2>Chromatic Symmetric Functions</h2>
<p>
  I work with Prof. Jeremy Martin (University of Kansas) on the chromatic symmetric function introduced by Stanley,
  studying the Stanley e-positivity conjecture and the van Willigenburg conjecture. This began with writing a survey paper as a project for my graph theory course, taught by Dr. Reuven Hodges.
</p>

<h2>Quantum Knot Invariants — SURIEM REU</h2>
<p>
  This summer I'll be at Michigan State University's SURIEM REU, supervised by postdoc
  Matthew Harper. The project focuses on combinatorial approaches to quantum sl₃ knot invariants, where we aim to find formulae for the 4th and 5th coefficients of sl₃ for positiver alternating link diagrams.
</p>
