<h1>Research</h1>

<h2>Interests</h2>
<p>
  My research sits at the intersection of algebraic combinatorics and representation theory.
  I'm particularly drawn to symmetric function theory — integer partitions, Young tableaux,
  and the rich structure connecting combinatorics to algebra.
</p>

<h2>Chromatic Symmetric Functions</h2>
<p>
  I work with Prof. Jeremy Martin (University of Kansas) on chromatic symmetric functions (CSFs),
  studying the Stanley e-positivity conjecture and the van Willigenburg conjecture.
  My work includes computational exploration of caterpillar graphs in SageMath and an original
  conjecture about leaf placement and e-positivity.
</p>

<h2>Quantum Knot Invariants — SURIEM REU</h2>
<p>
  This summer I'll be at Michigan State University's SURIEM REU, supervised by postdoc
  Matthew Harper. The project focuses on combinatorial approaches to quantum sl₃ knot invariants,
  including MOY calculus and the Temperley–Lieb algebra.
</p>

<h2>Graph Theory</h2>
<p>
  I worked with Prof. Reuven Hodges (University of Kansas) on a graph theory project covering
  Stanley's 1995 CSF paper, including the acyclic orientations theorem and its connection to
  the e-basis expansion.
</p>
